The reason your phone can recognise faces goes back to one contest in 2012 🧠

In 2012 three researchers in Toronto trained a deep neural network on two gaming graphics cards and crushed the ImageNet image-recognition contest. AlexNet showed that deep learning on GPUs works, and it shaped much of the AI that followed. Did you know gaming GPUs kicked off the AI boom?
