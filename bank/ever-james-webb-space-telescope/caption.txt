Every picture this telescope takes is a photo of the past 🔭

Webb can see light from about 180 million years after the Big Bang, when the first galaxies were forming. It does it with a gold mirror almost three times wider than Hubble's, kept colder than −223 °C, parked 1.5 million km from Earth. It started as a US$1 billion idea in 1999 and ended up costing US$10 billion. Worth it?
