Every time you pair your earbuds, you're saying a Viking king's name 🎧

Bluetooth started at Ericsson in 1989 as a way to build wireless headsets. Its name comes from the 10th-century Danish king Harald Bluetooth, suggested as a codename by an engineer who'd been reading about Vikings. It stuck, and now it connects earbuds, speakers and cars everywhere.
