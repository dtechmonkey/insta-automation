The great-grandparent of your laptop was nicknamed a "Giant Brain" 🖥️

ENIAC, finished in 1945, was the first programmable, electronic, general-purpose computer. Built for the US Army, it was about a thousand times faster than earlier machines, cost $487,000, and the press nicknamed it a 'Giant Brain'. It ran until 1955.
