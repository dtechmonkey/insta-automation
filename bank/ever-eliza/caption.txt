Sixty years before ChatGPT, people were already confiding in a chatbot 🖥️

In the 1960s, MIT's Joseph Weizenbaum built ELIZA, a program that pretended to be a therapist by reflecting your words back as questions. It understood nothing, yet many users were convinced it did, which became known as the ELIZA effect. Do you think today's chatbots really understand us?
