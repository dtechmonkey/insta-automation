The computer that took humans to the Moon was about as powerful as an early home computer 🚀

MIT built the Apollo Guidance Computer in the 1960s. It was the first computer made from silicon chips, fitted into about one cubic foot, and its software was literally woven into rope memory. Its power was close to an Apple II, yet it helped fly astronauts to the Moon. Wild, right?
