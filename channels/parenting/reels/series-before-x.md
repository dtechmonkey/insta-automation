# "Before X, try this" series (from 10 Nov 2026)

**Why this series:** the pencil reel r030 did best for us. It also copies the biggest reel in our research ("Before giving your child a pencil": 21.2M views, 759K likes).
It works for three reasons:
- It names a goal every parent already has.
- It gives 5 things to do at home today with kitchen items.
- It's easy to save and share.

It also sits closest to the book: Montessori activities for ages 2–4.

## The formula (copy r030 exactly)

| Part | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–5 s | "Before [goal], try this." Big text, child's hands busy. |
| Promise | 5–6 s | "Five things from your kitchen." |
| 5 activities | ~3.5 s each | Number on screen (1–5), one close-up of small hands per activity, 3–5 words of text. |
| Close | 4–5 s | One safety line plus the message: "No worksheets. Just [skill]." |
| End card | 3 s | Comment KEYWORD |

**Rules:**
- **Items:** Indian household items only (atta, katori, steel plate, dal, chappals, dupatta).
- **Activities:** every one is safe for ages 2–4, and each reel includes one safety line.
- **No promises:** say "builds" or "practises", never "makes her read early" or "guarantees".
- **Positive message:** everyday play is the real preparation.
- **Evidence:** each reel cites one public guide, like r030 cited the NHS occupational-therapy sheets. The source links below must be found and checked before recording. I've named only the kind of source, so nothing here is a citation yet.
- **Length and voice:** about 28–32 s, warm Indian English voiceover (Nika), Gemini-made music, so boosting stays possible.

## Production and cost
r030 used 5 clips. The cheaper way: generate the 5 hand close-ups as free stills and animate them as **6-second silent clips at 10 credits each**. Each reel has 5 activities plus a closing shot, so it costs about **50–60 credits**, and the voiceover is free.

To save more, use a slow zoom on stills for 2 of the 5 activities. That brings a reel to about 30–40 credits. It's slightly less lively, so test one reel first.
