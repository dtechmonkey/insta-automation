## Limits of this data

- Instagram does not show private sends. Reposts are the only public sharing number, so "shares" here means reposts.
- As far as I know reposts are a newer Instagram feature, so low counts on reels from 2023 to early 2025 may just reflect their age. Compare reposts only between recent reels.
- Search results are personalised and favour winners. This shows what can work, not how often it works.
- Mostly first frames and captions. I did not hear audio or watch reels through, because seeking froze the browser.
