# Let Me Try: 30 reel scripts (r003-r032)

Written 3 Nov 2026 from the research in `research-2026-11-03.md`. All are for ages 2-4 and set in Indian homes.

## Rules used in every script

- **Every reel ends on a positive parenting message.** Each one lists its message, and comedy has to teach something too. The rushed moments in Format A exist only to set up the better option.
- **Same parent, two moments.** Competitors show a "bad mom" and a "good mom". Top comments push back ("I'm both and it's ok"), so ours show one parent on a rushed day and a calmer day. This also keeps our no-shaming rule.
- **The first 2 seconds carry the hook:** conflict on screen, plus one large line of text.
- **Answer the objection inside the reel.** Comments said "he grabs a hot kettle", "after 2 more slides he won't go" and "you have to repeat it 10,000 times".
- **Comment keyword = a printable that already exists** in `docs/printables`.
- **Never shown:** a parent hitting a child, a child in danger, religion, caste or politics.

## Evidence grades

- **A:** a named study or official guidance appears in the script.
- **B:** reputable guidance, or a study I only saw through a summary. Read the original before posting.
- **C:** no research claim is made on screen. It is ordinary practice or opinion.

# Format A: Two Moments (15-17 s)

Clip 1 is the rushed moment, clip 2 the calmer one, followed by a 1.5 s closing line. Top label on clip 1: **RUSHED DAY**. Top label on clip 2: **SAME HOME. CALMER DAY.**
Adult lines can use Omni's native speech. Omni speech on a toddler hasn't been tried, so child lines use AI Studio TTS, as in the free-sample ad. Check that Hindi words in the adult lines sound natural on the first take.

## r017 · Why she keeps throwing food
**Proof:** "Why Your Toddler Throws Food" 2.5M views. **Evidence:** B. **Keyword:** PLATE
- **Positive message:** She's learning, not misbehaving, and calm repetition teaches her.
- **Hook text:** THROWN ON PURPOSE?
- **Voiceover:** "Third time today. The roti's on the floor. And she's watching your face. She isn't being naughty. At this age, children test what happens next. - **Shots:** (1) Roti falls; child of about 2 peers over the tray. (2) Her eyes on her mother's face. (3) Mother calmly moves the plate. (4) Plate returned; child takes a bite.
- **Caption:** It feels personal, but it's usually an experiment. Comment PLATE for a simple way to divide the mealtime jobs. Offer food in safe sizes and sit with her while she eats.
- **Hashtags:** #toddlermeals #toddlerbehavior #montessori #parentingtips #letmetry
