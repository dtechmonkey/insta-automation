# Let Me Try — 8-week content plan

**Dates (tentative):** Mon 19 Oct – Sun 13 Dec 2026, in 4 fortnights
**Posts:** 2 a day (28 per fortnight, 112 total)
- **1:00 PM:** practical posts (activities, how-tos)
- **7:30 PM:** mostly debate posts, because evening is when people comment and share most

**Mix:** 11 debate posts (39%) and 17 practical or warm posts (61%).

## Rules for debate posts
1. **Challenge the habit, never the parent.** The tone is "We all did this. Here's what works better."
2. **Every claim needs a basis** (listed below): research, AAP or WHO guidance, or Montessori principles.
3. **Off-limits:** religion, caste, politics, gender battles, and medical diagnosis.
4. **The last slide always asks a question** ("Agree or disagree? 👇"), so the argument happens in the comments, not in our tone.
5. **Psychology and health posts** carry a caption line saying they are general information, not medical advice.

## Hook formats we rotate
| Format | Cover pattern |
|---|---|
| Unpopular opinion | "Unpopular opinion: ___" |
| Stop / Start | "Stop saying ___. Say this instead." |
| Then vs. now | "What our parents did → what we know now" |
| Myth vs. fact | "Myth: ___ / Fact: ___" |
| Agree or disagree | A bold statement plus "Agree or disagree?" |
| Numbered list | "5 things your 2-year-old can do alone" |
